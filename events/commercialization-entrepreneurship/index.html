Practical Learning

Make it. Refine it. Build its value.

A focused, hands-on introduction to transforming local fruits into appealing products with commercial potential.

01 · PROCESS

Local fruit processing

Explore practical processing techniques for pineapple, banana, mango and other locally available fruits.

02 · CREATE

Jam and cordial

Learn the foundations of developing flavourful fruit spreads and drinks beyond high-sugar commercial options.

03 · DIVERSIFY

Candies and dried fruits

Discover alternative product formats that can extend a fruit's appeal, usability and potential shelf value.

04 · COMMERCIALIZE

Build a fruit-product business

Connect practical production skills with the entrepreneurial thinking needed to shape a marketable product.
