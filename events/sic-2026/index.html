About the symposium

Ideas move forward when they are made visible.

SIC 2026 creates a clear route for participants at different stages—from early concepts to tested innovations with commercialization potential.

The symposium brings UiTM innovators, students, UGUN participants, non-UiTM participants, and associated institutions into one structured showcase. Participants can enter through a dedicated UGUN category, present novice-stage concepts, or demonstrate advanced innovations ready for wider use.

Categories and fees

Choose the stage that matches your innovation.

Three entry routes keep evaluation relevant to the participant community and the maturity of each idea.

A

UGUN

UiTM-Global University Network

  • Dedicated participation route for UGUN participants.
  • Designed for the symposium's international network community.
Participation fee
UGUN participantsRefer organiser
B

Novice Innovation

For ideas and concepts still in development.

  • A fully functioning prototype is not mandatory.
  • Sketches, models, storyboards, mock-ups, simulations, or initial prototypes are accepted.
  • No comprehensive user validation is required.
  • The innovation has not been used in actual operations.
Participation fees
UiTM participantsRM35
Non-UiTM participantsRM50
C

Advanced Innovation

For tested work with wider application potential.

  • Has a working prototype or application.
  • Has undergone user testing or validation.
  • Shows potential for industrial use or commercialization.
  • May include intellectual property protection.
Participation fees
UiTM participantsRM50
Non-UiTM participantsRM80
