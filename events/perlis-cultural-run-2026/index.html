The Culture Stride experience

One route.
Many cultures.

Culture Stride turns a 5KM community run into a vivid meeting point for sport, friendship, art, and Malaysian cultural expression. Whether you are a seasoned runner or joining for a relaxed fun run, the experience is designed to feel welcoming at the start, electric on the route, and memorable at the finish.

Move
as one.

Run, jog, or enjoy the route at your own rhythm—then bring your friends, family, and running crew into one shared celebration.

5KM of shared energy

A welcoming public-distance event shaped around movement, encouragement, and a strong finisher moment.

Culture in motion

Pesona Nusantara-inspired ornament, colour, and identity turn the race into more than a standard running event.

Open community

Open to the public, the run creates an easy meeting point for UiTM, visitors, families, and the wider community.

Race kit reveal

Wear the story.
Earn the finish.

Every participant receives an exclusive event shirt with elegant batik and ethnic-inspired motifs, plus a distinctive 5KM finisher medal designed as a lasting race-day collectible.
