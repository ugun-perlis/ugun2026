The Culture Stride experience

One route.
Many cultures.

Culture Stride turns a 5KM community run into a vivid meeting point for sport, friendship, and Malaysian cultural expression. It is designed to feel welcoming at the start, electric on the route, and memorable at the finish.

Move
as one.

Whether you run, jog, or enjoy the route at your own rhythm, the day is built around shared momentum and an open community spirit.

5KM of shared energy

A welcoming public-distance event shaped around movement, encouragement, and a strong finisher moment.

Culture in motion

Pesona Nusantara-inspired ornament, colour, and identity turn the race into more than a standard running event.

Open community

Open to the public, the run creates an easy meeting point for UiTM, visitors, families, and the wider community.
