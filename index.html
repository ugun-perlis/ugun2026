About the Camp

An international platform for exchange, innovation, and friendship.

Hosted in Perlis by UiTM Cawangan Perlis, the camp blends academic collaboration, cultural discovery, entrepreneurship learning, and community-based activities in one compact global experience.

UGUN Summer Camp 2026 is designed for participants who want to experience academic exchange, innovation activities, and the richness of Perlis culture.

The programme includes structured learning modules, signature events, cultural activities, community engagement, and international networking opportunities.
